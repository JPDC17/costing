A big redesign. New start screen listing all your jobs with their status; a Summary that tells you at a glance how the job is doing; separate Contract and Estimate pages; holdback tracking on progress draws (paid and outstanding are now net of holdback; older jobs keep no holdback until you set it); a filter box and compact rows on long tables; and auto-save, so unsaved work can be recovered if the app closes unexpectedly. There is now also an installer (JobCosting-Setup.exe) that opens much faster than the portable exe; get it from the Releases page.
